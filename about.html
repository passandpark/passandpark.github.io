NSW rules & useful links

Everything official, in one place

The key NSW learner rules, plus direct links to the official Service NSW and NSW Government pages.

120 hours

Learners under 25 log 120 supervised hours, including 20 at night, before the driving test.

✖️3

3-for-1 lessons

Each hour with a NSW-licensed instructor logs as 3 hours, for up to 10 instructor hours (30 logbook hours). Bonus hours count as daytime driving.

🎓

Safer Drivers Course

Under-25 learners with 50+ logbook hours can complete the course for 20 extra logbook hours (run by approved providers).
